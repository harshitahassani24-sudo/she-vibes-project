Mommi

A chat-based companion that answers in my mother's voice — so cooking dinner feels like having the best cook you know in the kitchen with you.

What it is

Mommi helps working women and hostellers cook healthy, home-style meals on any evening of the week — without the mental load that usually sits between a long workday and an empty kitchen.

You describe what is in your fridge and how much time you have. The agent replies with one specific dish, written in my mother's voice, with short and clear steps. Not a recipe app. Closer to a knowing aunt on the other end of a chat.

It is 9 PM on a Wednesday.

You open Mommi. You type: "I have paneer, two tomatoes, and twenty minutes."

The agent replies in a warm, unhurried voice with one specific dish you can make in that window, in five short steps.

Twenty minutes later, you have eaten something real, and you did not have to think hard to get there.

Who it is for

Working women in their twenties and thirties cooking for themselves or a small household. Hostellers and students in shared kitchens with limited time and equipment. Young professionals living alone in cities, who grew up watching someone cook for them and now find themselves in front of a fridge at 9 PM without that person beside them.

The quiet promise

The agent will not lecture about nutrition. It will not push affiliate links or sponsored ingredients. It will not build a profile of you over time.

It is designed to make you more capable in the kitchen, not more dependent on it — the way a real mother's teaching works.

"The most important file in this project is not the Python code. It is the Knowledge Base — my mother's wisdom, written in her voice. The Python is small and replaceable. The Knowledge Base is irreplaceable."

Where it is being built

Mommi is currently in development, built during the She Vibes 66-day builder cohort. The technical pipeline is working. The next three weeks are about recording conversations with my mother and translating her decades-old cooking system into something the agent can reason with — across voice, pantry rules, cooking logic, substitutions, dishes, and refusals.
